Counties

Draw counties

Pick the region, name the county, then draw it on the map. Anything past the region's edge is trimmed off. A town belongs to the county holding most of its land.

Borders

Draw nation borders

Fit to neighbouring claims New borders are trimmed where they overlap another nation, so claims tile together instead of stacking. The shared edge follows the neighbour's own outline exactly.

Type a nation, then draw its border. Draw several areas under the same name for territory that isn't joined up — they all count as that nation.
